04 / BEFORE YOU WANDER

A GOOD NIGHT.
A FEW NOTES.

Less of an itinerary.
More of a state of mind.

STAY
CURIOUS ↗

An independent student-made visual journal about Tokyo after dark. It brings together city atmosphere, district portraits, and Japanese car culture. Think of it as a mood board you can wander through.

Start with a feeling: Shinjuku for layered neon, Shibuya for motion and street style, or Akihabara for electric color and game culture. The district cards are editorial impressions, not a live itinerary or a list of current opening hours.

Slow down. Look for a reflection instead of a skyline, a doorway instead of a landmark, or a single color repeated along a street. If you take photographs, keep paths clear and ask before photographing someone up close.

The garage celebrates the visual side of Japanese car culture: silhouettes, thoughtful details, and personal expression. Open a card to read a short editorial on what makes each part of that culture interesting.

FROM THE NIGHT TOKYO GARAGE

01 / THE MIDNIGHT ICON

NISSAN SKYLINE R34

Sharp lines, a legendary twin-turbo inline-six, and an unmistakable midnight presence. From highway runs across the Bayshore Route to underground meetings under the fluorescent garage lights, the R34 GT-R represents an era of Japanese engineering where mechanical purity and aerodynamic poise created a lasting worldwide icon.

FROM THE NIGHT TOKYO GARAGE

02 / ANALOG SOUL

TOYOTA SUPRA MK4

A driver-focused cockpit, tactile switchgear, and the legendary 2JZ-GTE powerplant. The MK4 Supra is an uncompromising tribute to mechanical emotion—built in an era where driving was purely analog, responsive to every pedal stroke, and free of unnecessary digital filtering.

03 / BUILT TO BE YOURS

MAZDA RX-7

The lightweight twin-rotary layout, perfectly balanced 50:50 weight distribution, and flowing timeless body lines. The FD RX-7 is more than a sportscar—it is a personal canvas for its builder, demanding devotion, craftsmanship, and a deep appreciation for the art of tuning.
